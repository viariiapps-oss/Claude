Rules

1. One link only

The only link accepted when listing is the official Roblox experience URL (roblox.com/games/…). Discord, YouTube, TikTok, X, Instagram, Linktree, personal websites and custom URLs are rejected.

2. One external destination

A game page has exactly one external button: PLAY ON ROBLOX. Clicks are counted before redirecting, so you always know how much traffic BloxBid sent you.

3. Rank is what you pay

  • Positions are ordered purely by bid, highest first.
  • Outbidding requires at least the minimum difference over the position you are taking.
  • Ties are broken by whoever reached the amount first.

4. Daily reset

The TODAY board closes at 00:00 UTC. The game holding #1 at that moment is archived as that day's Daily Champion. The ALL-TIME board is never reset.

5. Group verification

Creator and group are read from the Roblox API and cannot be entered by hand. This is what keeps anyone from listing a game as someone else's studio.

FAQ

Is listing my game free?

Yes. Listing is free and puts you on the board. Bidding is optional and only decides how high you appear.

Is this advertising?

No. There are no campaigns, CPM, CPC, audiences, targeting or banners. There is one product: the leaderboard.

What happens when someone outbids me?

You drop one or more positions and see it immediately in your dashboard, including who passed you and by how much. You can raise your bid at any time.

Where does the game data come from?

Name, thumbnail, creator, group, description, genre, subgenre, players, visits and favorites are read from Roblox. Rank, bid and clicks are BloxBid's own metrics.

Do clicks affect my rank?

No. Clicks are reporting for you, not a ranking signal. Only the bid moves you up.

Are you affiliated with Roblox?

No. BloxBid.lol is an independent discovery platform and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Roblox Corporation.

Privacy

We collect the minimum needed to run a public leaderboard: the public Roblox data of listed experiences, aggregate click counts on PLAY ON ROBLOX buttons, and basic account details for developers who list a game.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not build advertising profiles — there is no ad product to build them for.

Terms

By listing a game you confirm you are authorised to represent it. Bids buy a position on a public leaderboard for as long as no one outbids you; they are not a guarantee of traffic, players or revenue.

We may remove listings that break the link rules, impersonate another creator, or point to experiences removed from Roblox.
